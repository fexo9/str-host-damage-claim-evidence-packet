## The decision flow (excerpt from the full quick-start guide)

**Step 1 — Is the damage real and from this stay?**
Damage you can see and tie to this stay (photo + booking record) →
continue. Pre-existing damage, normal wear, or damage you cannot tie to
the stay → do not file.

**Step 2 — File or absorb?**
Absorb when ALL of these are true: the amount is small; you lack
before-photos AND have no other proof the item was intact; the
documentation burden exceeds the likely recovery.
File when: the amount is material to you, AND you have before + after
photos, AND you have a receipt, quote, or replacement-price basis.

**Step 3 — Document first (before cleaning or repair).**
Wide shot, close-up, timestamp ON, before-photo. Log each item. Build the
amount basis (quote / receipt / comparable listing). Repair before
photographing destroys the evidence.

**Deadlines** (per Airbnb's Host Damage Protection terms, article 279,
verified 2026-10-17): file within **14 days of the guest's checkout**;
guest has **24 hours** to respond; if involving Support, submit supporting
documentation **within 14 days of the damage or loss**. Terms change —
re-check the current terms page before filing.
